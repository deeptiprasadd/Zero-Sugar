# P11 - repeat check of the hand codes (D-050)

40 videos coded on 15 September and on 9 October (Instagram 24, YouTube 16; 4 are AI-character Shorts). Same coder, about three weeks apart. Krippendorff's alpha, nominal; 'unsure' and blank are missing; 2,000 bootstrap resamples (seed 20261009). Bands fixed in D-050: >= 0.80 reliable, 0.667-0.80 tentative, below 0.667 weakly reliable.
